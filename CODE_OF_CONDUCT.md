# Code of Conduct

We want React Markdown Studio to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or opinions.

## Our standard

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful, patient and constructive, including when you disagree
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback on code and ideas
- Assuming good intent, and asking questions before drawing conclusions
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate or harmful.

Report concerns privately by opening a **private security advisory** on the repository's *Security* tab, or by contacting the maintainer, [Akash Negi](https://github.com/AKASHNEGI-github), through his GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially and fairly.
